The Four Laws of Behavior Change

  1. Make it obvious: Design your environment so visual cues trigger your positive habits.
  2. Make it attractive: Bundle a habit you need to do with an action you want to do.
  3. Make it easy: Reduce friction and start with habits that take less than two minutes to complete.
